ABOUT OUR SERVICES

Dedicated to Keeping Your Power Running

A sudden generator fault shouldn't bring your daily routine or business operations to a halt. From carburetor tuning and coil rewinding to routine oil changes and engine overhauls, we deliver reliable repairs that restore peak engine performance. We diagnose the root cause fast, fix it right, and ensure your generator starts on the very first pull.

OUR EXPERTISE

Complete Generator Care,
Engine To Electrical.

Full-spectrum servicing engineered to eliminate fuel issues, stabilize electrical current, and extend engine life.

Fuel System & Carburetion

Ultrasonic cleaning, jet adjustment, and carburetor rebuilds to stop fuel leaks, rough idling, and sudden engine stalling.

Alternator & AVR Servicing

Stator and rotor coil rewinding, AVR replacement, and fixing fluctuating, low, or absent power output.

Complete Engine Overhauls

Piston ring replacement, valve clearance calibration, and cylinder head repairs to restore engine compression and power.

Preventative Servicing

Scheduled oil flushes, spark plug replacement, air filter restoration, and comprehensive load-bank testing.
